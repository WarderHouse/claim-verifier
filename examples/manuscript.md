# Training access in Southeast Asia: a synthetic example

Employer-provided training matters for development outcomes. Zarestky (2024)
found that women's participation in employer training lagged men's by ten
percentage points. Regional programs have expanded steadily since the 1990s
(Garavan & Carbery, 2019). We follow the six-phase approach to thematic
analysis (Braun & Clarke, 2006). Earlier work established the field's
foundations (McLean, 2004).
